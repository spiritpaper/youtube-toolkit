# Finding outliers on YouTube

An **outlier** is a video that got far more views than its channel usually gets. A 1M-view video means little on a channel that averages 2M; it means a lot on a channel that averages 50K. Outliers show you topics, titles and formats that worked *beyond* a channel's normal audience, and those are the best ideas to learn from.

## Outlier badges on thumbnails

On YouTube's home page, search results, sidebar and channel pages, each thumbnail gets a badge such as **4.2x**: the video has 4.2 times the median views of its channel's recent videos of the same format (long-form or Shorts).

| Badge color | Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red | under 0.5x | Underperforming |
| Grey | 0.5–2x | Typical for the channel |
| Green | 2–5x | Outlier |
| Bright green | 5–10x | Strong outlier |
| Purple | 10x and up | Breakout |

Hover a badge to see the channel median, views and age. Too much noise? In the popup, under **On YouTube**, set **Show badges at** to "≥ 2x only".

## The video panel

When you watch a video, a **YT Toolkit** panel appears at the top of the sidebar (on Shorts it floats on the right):

- **Outlier score** and verdict, compared with the channel's median
- **Views per day / per hour**, channel median and average, like and comment rates, subscribers, views ÷ subscribers, age and the **channel trend** (growing, stable or declining)
- **Most replayed**: YouTube's replay heatmap with clickable peaks (jump to the moments viewers rewatch)
- Buttons:
  - **Save idea**: adds the video to Saved outliers (with a full stats snapshot)
  - **Follow**: track the channel ([guide](channels.md))
  - **Analyze channel**: the channel's recent videos ranked by outlier score
  - **Transcript**: read the captions, then download, save, or **Analyze script** ([guide](scripts-and-titles.md))
  - **Explore topic**: start a keyword rabbit hole from this video ([guide](keyword-explorer.md))
  - **Video web**: map what YouTube suggests around it ([guide](video-web.md))
  - **Comments**: audience questions and requests ([guide](scripts-and-titles.md#audience-questions))
- **My notes**: **Add note at 3:42** pins a note to the moment the video is at (you can change the time before saving; Ctrl+Enter saves, Esc cancels). Click a note's time to jump the video there. Notes work on any video, saved or not, and also appear in the [Script lab](scripts-and-titles.md#my-notes).

## Channel analysis

On any channel page, click **Analyze channel** (bottom right), or use the button in the video panel. You get the last 200 long-form videos and 100 Shorts, ranked by outlier score, with median and average views, uploads per week, like rate, trend and a filter for 2x / 5x / 10x. Star (☆) any video to save it, or click **Follow channel**.

## Saved outliers

Everything you save lands in **Saved outliers** (sidebar → Create). Each card shows:

- the outlier score, how the video ranks within its channel ("Top 3%"), score vs the channel *average*, the channel trend, like rate vs the channel's typical like rate
- views gained since you saved it (saved outliers are re-checked daily)
- the replay heatmap, transcript and AI breakdown when you have them
- your notes and tags

From a card you can **Start project**, open its **Video web** or **Comments**, run an **AI breakdown** ("why did this work?"), or **Analyze script**. Tick 2–4 cards and click **Remix selected** to combine them into new ideas ([AI guide](ai-assistant.md)).

Exports (top right):

- **Export CSV**: one row per saved outlier with ~50 columns of stats ([column reference](../reference/metrics.md#csv-columns))
- **Export history**: one row per outlier per day, for growth charts

## Tips

- Hunt with the **Small-channel hunt** preset in the keyword explorer: ≥2x, 100K+ views, ≤25K subscribers, last 3 months. Small channels winning means the *idea* worked, not the channel's fame.
- Compare like with like: Shorts are scored against the channel's Shorts, long-form against long-form.
- A brand-new video (under ~3 days) hasn't collected its views yet; its score will rise.
