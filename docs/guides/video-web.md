# Video web

See how YouTube connects videos. Starting from one video, the web shows the videos YouTube suggests next to it, then the ones suggested next to those. It reveals the viewer journey YouTube has mapped for an audience, and where your video could fit in.

Open it from **Video web** in the video panel, a keyword-explorer result, a saved outlier, Home, or the sidebar (paste a video link).

## Reading the map

- **Each circle is a video.** Size = views. Color = outlier tier (legend at the bottom left).
- **An arrow A → B** means YouTube suggests B on A's watch page. A **thick line** means the two suggest each other.
- The **center** (dark ring) is the video you started from.
- Labels appear for the center, the hubs and the top outliers. Hover any circle for its title and numbers.
- **Drag** the background to pan, **scroll** to zoom, **Reset view** to recenter, **List view** for the same data as a table.

**Thumbnails.** Hover any video (or focus it with Tab) to see a card with its thumbnail, title, channel, views and outlier score. Zoom in (scroll) and the dots become round thumbnails, ringed in their outlier color, so you can see what the videos look like without losing the shape of the web. Thumbnails load only for videos in view, and only once. **Reset view** zooms back out to the dots.

## Click a video

The side panel explains **why it's connected**: the suggestion rank (#1 = top of the sidebar), same channel, and shared topic words. It also lists which videos suggest it and which it suggests. From there:

- **Expand suggestions / More suggestions**: add the videos YouTube suggests on that video (5 more each click)
- **☆ Save idea**, **New web from here**, **Explore keyword**

## The insights

- **Hubs**: videos suggested from many others in the web. These are the center of the audience's journey, and the strongest signal of what this audience watches next.
- **Outliers in the web**
- **Topic clusters**: groups of connected videos on the same topic (click one to highlight it)
- **Words in outliers**: words that appear in this web's outliers far more than in its other videos
- **Channels in the web**
- **Ideas from this web (AI)**: the AI reads the hubs and clusters as a viewer journey, points out openings for smaller channels, and suggests videos that would slot into the web

## Cost and notes

- A new web reads about 9 watch pages for free and scores 40–50 videos: usually 100–150 API units. Expanding a video adds a few more.
- YouTube personalizes suggestions, so two maps of the same video can differ a little. The web is a sample of YouTube's suggestions at that moment, not a fixed fact.
- The 30 most recent webs are saved; delete them under **Settings → Storage & data**.
