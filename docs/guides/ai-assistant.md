# AI assistant

AI is optional and uses **your own key**. Every feature works without AI; AI adds the "why" and drafts the writing.

## Connect a provider

**Settings → AI assistant**:

| Provider | Get a key | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Gemini | [aistudio.google.com/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) | Has a free tier. Default model `gemini-flash-latest` (always the current Flash model). |
| OpenAI | [platform.openai.com/api-keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys) | Default `gpt-5.4-mini` |
| Anthropic Claude | [platform.claude.com/settings/keys](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys) | Default `claude-opus-5-5` |
| OpenRouter | [openrouter.ai/keys](https://openrouter.ai/keys) | Hundreds of models from many providers |

1. Choose the provider, paste the key, click **Connect**. Chrome asks permission to contact that provider (the extension can only reach providers you connect).
2. The model list loads from the provider; pick any model your key can use.
3. **Describe your channel**: one or two sentences about your niche, size and style. Every AI feature uses it to tailor ideas to you.

**Free vs paid Gemini**: it's the same kind of key either way. The tier comes from whether billing is on for the key's Google Cloud project. The free tier has lower rate limits, and Google may use free-tier prompts to improve its products. Keep that in mind before sending transcripts or comments.

## Where AI helps

| Feature | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI breakdown | Saved outliers card | Why the video outperformed (looks at the thumbnail, transcript and replay peaks), the repeatable formula, 5 titles for you |
| Generate ideas | Saved outliers | Patterns across your saved outliers and 10 ideas for your channel |
| Outlier remix | Saved outliers (tick 2–4) | Combines different outliers' formats into 5 new concepts |
| AI angles | Keyword explorer | Keywords to dig into next |
| Ideas from this web | Video web | Reads the viewer journey and suggests videos that fit into it |
| Find gaps with AI | Content gaps | Writes up the gaps with title ideas |
| Improve with AI | Title lab | Ranks your drafts and writes stronger titles |
| Script breakdown | Script analyzer | Hook, story beats, attention tactics, keywords, payoff, beat sheet |
| Outline my version | Script analyzer | A hook, beat sheet and re-hooks for your topic |
| Turn into video ideas | Audience questions | What viewers want, their pain points, 6 ideas |
| Draft outline | Project | Outline from your angle and linked outliers |
| Hook rewriter | Project | 5 new openings modelled on your hook library |
| Re-hook planner | Project | Lines to win viewers back at 25/50/75% |
| Shot list | Project | Shot table, filming checklist, B-roll and edit notes |
| Thumbnail critique | Project | Reviews your thumbnail against the outliers' thumbnails |

Results are saved (on the card, the project or under **Past AI results** on Saved outliers), so you don't pay for the same answer twice. Most results can become a project in one click.

## Good to know

- AI calls go **directly from your browser to the provider**. Nothing passes through any other server.
- A failed call (network blip, "model overloaded") is retried once automatically.
- Long scripts and image critiques can take 1–2 minutes.
- Treat AI output as a draft: check facts, and keep your own voice.
