# Idea board and projects

Saved outliers are other people's videos. The **Idea board** holds *your* videos, as cards moving through five columns:

**Idea → Planned → Scripting → Filming & editing → Published**

Drag cards between columns (or use the dropdown on each card). Click a card to open its **project workspace**.

## Start a project

- On the board, type an idea and click **Add**.
- On a saved outlier, click **Start project** (the outlier is linked as inspiration).
- On a content gap, click **Start project**.
- After an AI outline in the Script analyzer, an outlier remix, or audience research: **Save as project**.

## The project workspace

Everything about one video, top to bottom. Text fields save automatically as you type.

### The idea and inspiration

- **The idea**: your angle (what makes your version different, who it's for, the promise) and notes.
- **Inspiration**: the outliers this video is modelled on. Add any saved outlier from the dropdown. If an outlier has a saved transcript, **Script analysis** opens its breakdown.

### Titles and outline

- **Titles**: one candidate per line. **Test in Title lab →** opens them in the [Title lab](scripts-and-titles.md#title-lab).
- **Outline**: write it, or click **Draft outline with AI**. The AI uses your angle and your linked outliers (including their script breakdowns, if you've run them) to write title options, a word-for-word hook, a beat sheet with re-hooks and the payoff.
- **Edit / Preview**: the Outline and Shot list are Markdown. **Preview** shows them formatted (headings, tables, lists); **Edit** goes back to the text. Double-click the preview to edit. The box switches to Preview after an AI draft, and remembers the view you last picked.

### Script and Script check

Write or paste your script; the header shows word count and estimated length. Then **Script check**:

1. Set your speaking **pace** (words per minute; ~150 is conversational).
2. Optionally pick an outlier to **compare with** (needs a saved transcript).
3. Click **Check script**. You get:
   - **Hook**: your first 30 seconds and which hook signals it uses
   - **Re-hook checkpoints** at 25%, 50% and 75% of the runtime: the line spoken there, and whether an attention device (tease, twist, question, stakes, story beat) lands nearby. ⚠ marks the weak ones.
   - **Drift zones**: stretches of a minute or more with no attention device, where viewers are most likely to drift
   - **Comparison** with the outlier: length, hook words, hook signals, attention devices per minute (by type), "you" per minute. Red marks where you're well below the outlier.

**Hook rewriter (AI)**: 5 new openings (50–90 words, ready to read aloud), each using a different technique (curiosity gap, stakes up front, contrarian claim, cold open, result first), modelled on the hooks in your [hook library](scripts-and-titles.md#hook-library).

**Re-hook planner (AI)**: for each checkpoint, two lines that pull viewers back in, quoted against the sentence they go before, plus other spots where the energy drops.

### Shot list

- **Starter from script**: one shot per paragraph (or per three sentences) with A-roll, B-roll and on-screen text slots.
- **Generate with AI**: a full shot table (beat, shot type, what to film, on-screen text, location and props), plus a filming checklist, generic B-roll and retention notes for the edit.
- **Download** (Markdown) or **Print** (prints the formatted version).

### Thumbnail

- **Upload thumbnail** (JPG or PNG; stored at up to 1280 px wide). Once the video is published and linked, its live thumbnail is used if you haven't uploaded one.
- The **mock feed** shows it next to your linked outliers, in YouTube's dark or light feed, at full size and at phone size. Can you tell what the video is at a glance, next to the competition?
- **Critique with AI**: the AI looks at your thumbnail *and* the outliers' thumbnails, then reviews first impression, readability at small size, curiosity, what the outliers do differently, 3 fixes and 3 alternative concepts, with a 1–10 score. Image critiques take 1–2 minutes.

### After publishing

Paste your published video link and click **Start tracking** (the project moves to Published). The video is tracked for 30 days, every ~4 hours in the first week and then daily:

- **Views now** and **outlier score** against your channel's median
- views and score **at 48 hours** and **at 7 days** (link the video on publish day to capture these)
- like rate, a views-over-time chart, and the outliers it was modelled on, for comparison
- **Signed in** ([My channel](my-channel.md)): your real **retention curve**, average % viewed, average view duration, subscribers gained and, where the API provides it, thumbnail **click-through rate**

## Tips

- Link 2–3 outliers as inspiration before drafting the outline; the AI uses their structure.
- Run **Script check** after every big rewrite; drift zones are the fastest wins.
- Upload two thumbnail options in turn and compare them in the mock feed before you publish.
