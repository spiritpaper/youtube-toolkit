# Channels you follow

Follow channels you want to learn from (and your competitors) to track them over time. Followed channels power four pages: **Following**, **Compare**, **Radar** and **Content gaps**.

## Follow a channel

Any of these:

- In the YouTube video panel, click **Follow**.
- On a channel page, click **Analyze channel → Follow channel**.
- On the **Following** page, paste an `@handle` or channel link, choose **Learn from** or **Competitor**, and click **Follow**.

After following, check the card shows the channel you meant: a mistyped handle can belong to another channel.

## Following

One card per channel:

- subscribers and their **30-day change**, **median views** (long-form and Shorts), uploads per week, **hit rate** (share of recent videos at 2x+ the channel's median) and **trend**
- the **latest uploads** with their outlier scores
- **Growth & notes**: subscriber and median-view charts from the daily history, plus your own notes ("what to learn from this channel")

Filter by **Learn from / Competitors**, sort by latest upload, growth, median views or hit rate. **Refresh now** updates every channel immediately (~5 units each); otherwise it happens once a day automatically.

How tracking works: once a day the extension refreshes each followed channel's recent uploads. The same refresh records a snapshot (subscribers, total views, median views) for the growth charts **and** checks for new outliers for the Radar, so tracking costs nothing extra.

## Compare

A side-by-side table of your channel and every followed channel, using the same public math for all (so the comparison is fair): subscribers, median views (long-form and Shorts), uploads per week, hit rate, trend, views per subscriber and like rate. Click a column header to sort. Below the table, plain-language lines compare you with the competitor median, for example "Your median views per video is 0.5x the competitor median".

To include yourself, sign in on **My channel**, or set your channel under **Not signed in? Compare as:**.

## Radar

New outliers, delivered daily:

- **From channels you follow**: uploads from the last 14 days that already have 2x+ their channel's median views.
- **From keywords you follow** (🔔): new videos from the last 30 days in that keyword's search results that are 2x+ their channel's median.

The count shows on the extension icon and in the sidebar. **Check now** runs the check immediately; **Mark all read** clears the badge. Filter by channels or keywords.

### Keyword alerts

Follow a keyword with **🔔 Alert me** in the keyword explorer, or **Watch keyword** on the Radar page (up to 15). Each is checked daily: the search page is free; only new, recent videos are scored (about 1 unit plus ~5 per uncached channel). Videos already in the results when you follow a keyword aren't reported.

## Content gaps

Topics that keep producing outliers for the channels you follow, but that you haven't covered.

- The extension collects the 2x+ outliers among your followed channels' recent uploads and extracts their topics (words and two-word phrases).
- It keeps topics that are much more common in outliers than in those channels' ordinary uploads ("lift"), so niche-wide words like "travel" drop out ([formula](../reference/metrics.md#content-gaps)).
- It removes topics already in your own recent titles (sign in, or set "compare as" on Compare).

Each gap shows its evidence videos (save them with ☆), the channels, and buttons to **Explore keyword** (start a rabbit hole) or **Start project** (Idea board). **Find gaps with AI** writes the gaps up with title ideas for your channel.
