AI assistant optional

Use your own key from any provider to get breakdowns of why an outlier worked and fresh video ideas from your saved outliers. Keys are stored only in this browser and sent only to the provider you choose.

Runs on this computer: free, private, no key. Ollama uses http://localhost:11434; LM Studio uses http://localhost:1234 (start its server first).

Each request only uses as much as it needs, up to this. A bigger window uses more memory; if the model gets slow or fails to load, lower it.

Tracking saved ideas

Costs about 1 YouTube API unit per 50 ideas, plus about 5 per channel whose data isn't already cached.

Idea board

Archived videos leave the board but keep everything, including their final numbers, in the board's Archive view. Tracking after publishing runs for 30 days either way.

Restore this backup?

    Nothing is deleted. Items you already have are merged, keeping the newer copy; followed channels combine their history. API keys are never in a backup.

    Backups include saved outliers and their history, projects and thumbnails, followed channels with their stats and growth history, the radar and keyword alerts, rabbit holes, video webs, transcripts, heatmaps, comment research, AI results and your AI settings, but never your API keys or Google sign-in.

    Version & updates

    You're running .

    To update: unzip the new version over this extension's folder (same place, so your data stays). The extension notices the new files and reloads itself within a few minutes, once no Outlier Lab page is open. Or let a script do it: on Windows, double-click scripts\update.cmd in the extension's folder; on a Mac or Linux, run sh scripts/update.sh . inside it.
