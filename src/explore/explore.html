Find outliers by keyword, one level at a time

  1. Start with a broad topic. Every result is scored against its own channel's median.
  2. Check the opportunity score: lots of outliers, small channels winning and fresh demand are good signs.
  3. Click a related keyword to dig a level deeper, star the good ones, save outlier videos as ideas, and click the trail to climb back up.

Searching is free (it reads YouTube's own search page). Scoring uses your YouTube API key: about 1 unit per keyword plus ~5 per new channel (channels are cached for 12 hours).
